# NodeThemes AI Chat

**Free AI chatbot plugin for WordPress.**

NodeThemes AI Chat is a free AI chatbot plugin for WordPress. It answers your customers in a live chat on your website, using your own OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini API key and what you tell it about your business. Questions it cannot answer are passed to you.

- Website: [NodeThemes AI Chat on nodethemes.com](https://nodethemes.com/product/nodethemes-ai-chat-pro/)
- All free plugins: [Free WordPress plugins from NodeThemes](https://nodethemes.com/free-plugins/)

## Features (free version)

- Live chat widget on your website
- Answers with your own OpenAI, Claude or Gemini API key
- Describe your business in plain words, plus a Knowledge list of questions and answers
- Never invents prices or policies: unknown questions go to you ("Needs you" in the Inbox, plus an e-mail)
- Inbox: read every conversation, reply yourself, switch the AI off per conversation
- 50 AI replies per month

## NodeThemes AI Chat Pro

The free plugin is complete on its own. The Pro add-on, sold at [nodethemes.com](https://nodethemes.com/product/nodethemes-ai-chat-pro/) as a yearly license with updates and support, adds:

- Unlimited AI replies
- Telegram bot and Telegram Business
- WhatsApp (official Cloud API, or your own number by QR code)
- Human mode: typing indicator and natural reply timing
- Learns from your answers
- Questions sent to your Telegram
- WooCommerce product answers and a backup AI provider

Pro is installed next to the free plugin; your settings and data stay when you upgrade. See the full **Free vs Pro** comparison table on the [product page](https://nodethemes.com/product/nodethemes-ai-chat-pro/).

## Installation

1. Download the latest version from [nodethemes.com/free-plugins](https://nodethemes.com/free-plugins/). You can also click **Code → Download ZIP** above, but the version on nodethemes.com is ready to upload as it is.
2. In WordPress go to **Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin**, choose the zip and click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
3. New versions then appear in **Dashboard → Updates** like any other plugin.

Requirements: see the plugin header and `readme.txt`.
